How to play

  • Roll the die and your hero moves that many spaces. Only the space you stop on counts.
  • Things going wrong send you back: running away or failing a trap returns you to the space you came from, a failed quest (fewer than 2 right) does the same, and being knocked out sends you back to the camp at the start of the level.
  • With Lucky skips on, rolling a 5 or 6 lets you skip a battle, trap, quest or blitz. Guardians can never be skipped.
  • Every encounter is won by solving maths. Wrong answers show the full working so you learn from them.
  • Get answers right in a row to build a combo. Combos make your attacks stronger.
  • Each region ends with a Guardian. Defeat it to open the next region.
  • Type fractions like 3/4. Units and £ signs are optional.
  • Beat a Guardian to unlock that region in the Challenge Hall. Each challenge earns up to 3 stars, so there is always a better score to chase.
  • Bounties in the Challenge Hall refresh as you complete them.
  • Want extra practice? The Practice room lets you pick any topic or subtopic, choose word problems or quick questions, and practise with free hints and no monsters. The Skill book suggests what to practise next.
  • Quests and treasure chests often use word problems: read the story, pick out the numbers, then work step by step.
  • From any camp you can fast travel back to earlier regions. Chests in cleared regions refill each time you return.
  • Music loops all through the game and gets quieter whenever a sound effect plays. Tap the speaker button at the top to mute everything. Change effects and music separately, or choose your own sounds, in Menu → Hero.
  • Progress saves in this browser automatically.
