# Numeria Quest

**A maths adventure game for GCSE Foundation students.**

Students create a hero, roll the die to move along a board-game map, and battle monsters, unlock treasure chests and escape traps, all by solving maths problems. Every wrong answer shows the full working, so students learn as they play.

▶ **Play now:** https://favour-nine.github.io/numeria-quest/

---

## How it works

- **Board game:** roll the die to move along the road. Each space is an encounter: battle, treasure, trap, quest, timed blitz or camp.
- **Adventure:** six regions, one for each topic area, each guarded by a boss. Beat the Guardian to open the next region and reach the Castle of Infinity.
- **Action:** timed Blitz rounds, combo streaks and animated battles.
- **Role-playing progression:** choose a class (Knight, Mage or Ranger) and a character look, then earn XP, level up, collect gold and buy items.
- **Replay value:** the Challenge Hall has star-rated modes (Time Attack, Survival, Perfect Ten, Elite Rematch), plus bounties, achievements and hidden secrets.
- **Practice room:** students pick any topic or one of 81 subtopics, choose word problems, quick questions or a mix, set how many questions and what level, and practise with free hints and no monsters. The results screen shows a breakdown by subtopic and a "Retry my mistakes" option.
- **Word problems:** real-life, multi-step questions about wages, phone contracts, sales, recipes, travel, gardens, ladders, raffles and more. They also appear in quests and treasure chests.

## Topics covered

Questions are generated fresh every time, so no two games are the same. They follow the Edexcel GCSE Foundation specification:

| Region | Topic |
|---|---|
| Number Meadows | Number: place value, BIDMAS, primes, factors, powers, standard form, rounding and bounds |
| Fraction Forest | Fractions, decimals and percentages, including percentage change and interest |
| Algebra Caves | Equations, expanding and factorising, sequences, inequalities, graphs |
| Ratio River | Ratio, proportion, best buys, exchange rates, speed |
| Geometry Peaks | Angles, polygons, area, volume, Pythagoras, trigonometry, bearings |
| Statistics Citadel | Averages, charts, probability, tree diagrams |

Many questions include diagrams such as graphs, shapes, pie charts, Venn diagrams and timetables.

## Features for teachers

- **Difficulty settings:** Gentle, Standard or Exam ready.
- **Skill book:** shows each student's accuracy by topic and suggests what to practise next.
- **Lucky skips:** can be switched off if you want every encounter played.
- **Works on any device:** laptops, tablets and phones, with nothing to install.

## Saving progress

Progress saves automatically in the student's browser. It stays on that device and browser, and resets if browsing data is cleared.

## Running it yourself

The whole game is one file, `index.html`. Open it in any modern browser, or host it on any static web host.
